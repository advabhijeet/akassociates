## Answer in brief: where should a fresh MSME claim be filed?

For new delayed-payment applications, use the Ministry of MSME's [MSME ODR portal](https://odr.msme.gov.in/). The Ministry confirmed the change from Samadhaan in its parliamentary replies of 9 and 12 February 2026: fresh filings moved to ODR on 15 October 2025.

An online application is the beginning of a dispute-resolution process, not a finding that an invoice is payable. Before filing, identify the eligible supplier, the transaction, the correct Facilitation Council, the amount actually outstanding and the applicable statutory dates. Retain the original filing and subsequent notices.

This guide is based on published Ministry documents and the statutory framework reviewed on 27 November 2026. It is not a walkthrough of an authenticated portal account. Screen labels, upload constraints and individual Council instructions may differ; check the current official interface before submitting.

## MSME Samadhaan versus MSME ODR

| Question | Practical distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Where should a new application begin? | The current central ODR portal. |
| What about an earlier Samadhaan application? | Preserve its acknowledgement and case history; verify its current status and any migration direction. |
| Who determines disputed liability? | The competent Council or the dispute-resolution forum acting under the applicable legal framework, not the filing website itself. |
| Is a new filing needed for a pending case? | Do not create a duplicate reference merely because the portal changed. Confirm the existing case's status first. |

The filing channel changed in 2025. It should not be confused with the later MSMED Amendment Act, 2026 or treated as proof that every hearing must take place virtually.

## Step 1: verify supplier eligibility and the Council

The delayed-payment mechanism concerns qualifying micro and small suppliers of goods or services. Do not assume that every enterprise carrying an MSME or Udyam certificate has an enforceable claim under Chapter V.

Check the legal name of the enterprise, registration history, nature of its activity, constitution and authority to act. Compare the registration date against the contract and the supplies for which payment is claimed. A late registration or a transaction involving trading activity requires particular legal review. Eligibility may be contested even if the application is accepted by the portal.

Under the Section 18(4) framework, supplier-side territorial jurisdiction is central, even when the buyer is in another State. Establish the relevant supplier location and identify the competent Council rather than choosing a Council solely because the purchaser or delivery site is there.

See the [Udyam registration guide](/updates/msme-registration-benefits-eligibility.html) and [inter-State MSEFC jurisdiction guide](/updates/msefc-jurisdiction-supplier-buyer-different-states.html).

## Step 2: build an invoice-wise claim before opening the portal

A useful claim schedule separates amounts from dates. Start with the contract or work order, then reconcile delivery records, invoice values, deductions, credit notes, receipts and the balance still claimed.

| Field to prepare | Supporting record |
| --- | --- |
| Contract or order reference | Agreement, purchase order, work order and amendments |
| Supply or service date | Delivery challan, goods receipt, completion certificate or other performance record |
| Acceptance and objections | Written acknowledgment, inspection report, defect complaint or proof of rectification |
| Invoice amount and payment terms | Invoice and agreed written terms |
| Amount received and date | Bank entry, remittance advice or ledger supported by bank evidence |
| Credits and deductions | Credit notes, returned stock record and correspondence |
| Outstanding principal and interest | Reconciliation and separate calculation sheet |
| Limitation and prior proceedings | Due dates, acknowledgments, payments, previous filings and orders |

Section 15 must be applied using the acceptance or deemed-acceptance record: a written credit term is subject to a 45-day statutory ceiling; absent a written agreement, payment is required before the appointed day. Section 16 prescribes interest at three times the RBI Bank Rate, compounded with monthly rests. Do not substitute the repo rate, a flat contractual rate or a single present-day Bank Rate for the applicable historical calculation.

A timely written objection to acceptance may affect the statutory date. An unpaid invoice does not, by itself, establish that delivery, acceptance or performance is undisputed. See the [payment deadline guide](/updates/msme-45-days-payment-rule.html), [interest calculation guide](/updates/msme-interest-calculation-documents-caution-points.html) and [legal tools](/legal-tools.html).

## Step 3: prepare the documents and application particulars

The Ministry scheme describes electronic registration and submission using supplier registration information. Its indicative particulars include party details, claim grounds, supporting documents, receipts and the remaining amount due; representative authority and identity are relevant where a representative acts.

Prepare a compact set of readable PDFs, retain editable originals and follow the actual portal requirements. Suggested folders:

- A: enterprise registration, constitution and authority.
- B: contract and work-order chronology, including amendments.
- C: invoices and delivery/performance evidence.
- D: buyer correspondence, admissions, written objections and return records.
- E: bank records, ledger, credit notes, principal and interest reconciliation.
- F: limitation chronology and disclosures concerning earlier proceedings.

If no formal purchase order exists, preserve correspondence, delivery records, accepted invoices and other evidence proving the underlying transaction. Do not treat a legacy Samadhaan FAQ about affidavits or combined PDF uploads as a verified current ODR upload rule.

Where several work orders or projects are involved, build a separate schedule for each contract first. Whether claims can be grouped is a legal and procedural question; a portal's ability to accept multiple attachments does not resolve it.

## Step 4: file and preserve the submission record

Open the official ODR portal, use the supplier filing/login facility available there, verify the registration-linked particulars and enter the correct buyer and claim details. Attach the relevant supporting material, review all declarations and reconcile the amount entered with the underlying schedule. Do not invent a date merely to satisfy a required field; where the correct date or upload requirement is unclear, verify the Council's directions before final submission.

Save the submitted application, uploaded PDFs, payment calculation as filed, acknowledgement, reference identifiers, time of submission and subsequent communications. A later corrected schedule should be saved as a separate version, not silently substituted for the original filing.

The Ministry's Lok Sabha reply of 12 February 2026 identifies documentary deficiencies, inconsistent information, registration issues, delayed responses and Council workload among the reasons for pre-admission pendency. An organised claim record helps address avoidable deficiencies, although it cannot guarantee admission or a particular disposal date.

## Step 5: understand the voluntary pre-MSEFC stage

The scheme guidelines envisage optional digital assessment and negotiation before formal Council proceedings. Their indicative process allows parties to opt out; the initial pre-Council period is 15 days, with a consensual extension of no more than another 15 days. A short initial buyer-response window may apply under the scheme (three days from the automated notice); check the notice actually received rather than assuming that this is a universal deadline for a formal statement of defence.

An indicative assessment is not an arbitral award. A negotiated settlement should identify invoices, principal, interest, payment dates, default consequences and the treatment of any remaining disputed items. Preserve the executed settlement and payment proof.

## Step 6: follow the formal MSEFC reference

Where the preliminary process is skipped or unsuccessful, follow the formal reference. Under the applicable Section 18 framework, conciliation precedes arbitration; if conciliation fails and terminates, the dispute may move to arbitration by the Council or a referred institution. Comply with actual directions and check whether the proceeding is at scrutiny, conciliation or arbitration.

Section 18(5) sets a 90-day statutory period for deciding a reference. Do not read this as a guarantee that payment or an award will be received within 90 days of clicking submit. Preserve separate dates for the application, reference, notices, commencement, termination of conciliation and award. These may matter for limitation or a subsequent procedural objection.

See the [MSEFC process guide](/updates/msme-facilitation-council-process.html) and [limitation guide](/updates/msme-limitation-period-section-18-conciliation-arbitration.html).

## What should a buyer do after receiving an ODR notice?

Identify the actual stage and response deadline. Download the claim and supporting documents, preserve the notice and service record, and compare the amounts claimed with the buyer's own accounts.

A buyer should separately address supplier eligibility, territorial jurisdiction, limitation, admitted payments, quality or quantity disputes and interest. Defective goods, returned material or contractual deductions need evidence; an assertion in a reply is not proof of a set-off. Preserve the right to raise a properly pleaded counterclaim where maintainable.

A response submitted at the preliminary negotiation stage may not satisfy later arbitral pleading directions. File the appropriate formal response when required. See [MSEFC buyer reply and defences](/updates/msefc-notice-buyer-reply-defences-documents.html) and [defective goods, set-off and counterclaims](/updates/msefc-defective-goods-set-off-counterclaim.html).

## How to track a pending reference or migrated Samadhaan case

Keep a dated case log with the old application number, current reference or case number, assigned Council, date of notice, current stage, latest order, next date and required action. If an older case is not visible in the new account, check the previous acknowledgement and contact the concerned Council using its current official contact details. Do not assume that nonvisibility means dismissal or that refiling preserves the earlier filing date.

If the portal and an official notice conflict, preserve both and seek clarification promptly. Follow the actual Council's directions regarding hearing mode, service, fees, affidavits or physical copies. National digital filing does not eliminate the need to comply with applicable State procedure.

## After an award

Receiving an award is a separate stage from recovering money. Check service of the signed award, challenge limitation, any applicable Section 19 deposit and whether a stay order actually exists. An award challenge is not automatically a stay of enforcement. Refer to the [post-MSEFC award guide](/updates/after-msefc-award-challenge-deposit-stay-enforcement.html) for the separate remedies.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I still use Samadhaan for a new case?

Fresh central online filings moved to ODR on 15 October 2025. Keep existing Samadhaan records and confirm migration or Council directions for older matters.

### Is every claim eligible simply because Udyam registration is valid?

No. The nature and timing of supplies, supplier status and relevant law must be checked independently.

### Does the ODR portal guarantee payment within 45 days?

No. Section 15 regulates payment obligations. It does not guarantee recovery within that period after filing.

### What if a buyer refuses to negotiate?

The preliminary negotiation stage is voluntary. An unresolved dispute may proceed through the applicable formal Council process.

### Can a supplier file again when an old case is missing from the new account?

Do not duplicate a pending reference without first resolving its status. Retain original acknowledgements and obtain clarification from the Council.

## Current statutory transition

The MSMED (Amendment) Act, 2026 (Act No. 16 of 2026), section 1(2), permits phased commencement. S.O. 5575(E) dated 12 October 2026 expressly appoints 15 October 2026 for sections 2–7, 11 and 13–17, and 15 December 2026 for section 12 of the Amendment Act. It does not appoint commencement dates for sections 8, 9 or 10, which concern changes to section 18 (including online dispute resolution), proposed section 18A (enforcement) and substitution of section 19 (award challenges). The October 2025 portal transition is distinct from those statutory amendments. Do not apply the amended dispute-resolution timelines, land-revenue recovery provision or substituted pre-deposit provisions solely on the basis of the October 2026 notification. The principal Act and Mediation Act commencement position must be checked together with any later notifications and the Council's directions in the particular case.
