## Status definitions (16 October 2026)

- **Production:** implemented in the public website; do not infer that Google has indexed every URL.
- **Active operations:** maintained or executed as ongoing website, publishing, legal-review or SEO work.
- **Awaiting external evidence:** needs Search Console, analytics, approval or browser evidence rather than an unverified code-level claim.
- **Conditional:** implement only after specified approval, account status or search-demand checkpoint.
- **Deferred / planned:** not currently authorised as a production feature; not a blocker for routine article publication.
- **Historical:** preserves an older plan or checkpoint but must not override current operational documentation.
