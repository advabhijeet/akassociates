## Editorial and professional safeguards
BCI Rule 36 prohibits direct and indirect advertising/solicitation; website particulars exception is prescribed and limited. Government explanation: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2043470&lang=2&reg=48 ; Rules: https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_3_46_00001_196125_1517807320172&filename=BCI+Rules%2C+Part-V+to+IX.pdf&type=rule . Do not copy rival promotional pages, claims of superiority, guarantees, unverifiable experience or solicitations. Informational articles require professional-conduct review; disclaimer alone is not exemption. Publish original legal analysis, verified citations, genuinely useful tables/checklists and contextual internal links. No competitor copying or keyword-stuffed doorway pages.
